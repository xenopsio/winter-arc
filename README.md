# winter-arc

I'm using this repo to keep track of what I actually do during the Arc — mainly programming, cybersecurity, Linux, projects, and general skill development.

The main idea is simple:

Study → Understand → Document → Archive

I don't want this to be a collection of random notes or AI-generated stuff. It's here to show my actual progress over time, including things I struggled with, mistakes I made, things I learned, and what I can eventually do on my own.

What's in here
logs/

My daily logs.

They include what I worked on that day, what I learned, problems I ran into, and what I planned to work on.

reports/

For things that are worth documenting separately, such as bigger technical writeups, project milestones, or important things I've learned.

I won't make a report for everything. Most things will just stay in the daily logs.
